**TFA Constructions has previously held Federal Safety Accreditation, it has since lapsed,
and TFA is "currently progressing the renewal process".** Tariq's own words, in writing to
Kaunitz Yeung Architecture, 18 Sep 2026 11:10am.

How it surfaced: Kaunitz Yeung asked on **31 Aug 2026** for TFA's current Federal Safety
Accreditation certificate for the **Health + Wellbeing Hub Dakabin QLD** Expression of
Interest. It sat for over a fortnight. Tariq chased Kendal for a draft response on 9 Sep
3:23pm and again on **18 Sep 9:32am** ("how did we go with a draft response below for
this?"), then sent it himself an hour and a half later.

What he asked them: **can the EOI proceed on that basis, with the renewed certificate
provided as part of the tender process.** Unanswered as at 18 Sep 2026. If they say no,
the Dakabin EOI is gone.

Why this matters beyond one EOI: the Office of the Federal Safety Commissioner scheme is
the gate on Commonwealth-funded building work generally, so a lapsed accreditation is a
standing disqualifier on that whole class of tender, not a single missing document. It
also cuts against the pipeline he is building in education, health and community work
(see [[projects]], Tenders and prospects).

**How to apply:** treat "is the FSA renewal done" as a live open item until something says
otherwise. If any tender, EOI or prequalification asks for federal safety accreditation,
flag this immediately rather than letting it be answered casually. Two things are worth
putting in front of him: the answer from Kaunitz Yeung, and who at TFA actually owns the
renewal, which nothing read so far establishes.
