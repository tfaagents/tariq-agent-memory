## Why it matters beyond one EOI
The Office of the Federal Safety Commissioner scheme is the gate on Commonwealth-funded
building work generally, so a lapsed accreditation is a standing disqualifier on that whole
class of tender, not a single missing document. It also cuts against the pipeline he is
building in education, health and community work (see [[projects]], Tenders and prospects).
Dakabin is the proof. (seen 1 Oct 2026)

**How to apply:** treat "is the FSA renewal done" as a live open item until something says
otherwise. If any tender, EOI or prequalification asks for federal safety accreditation,
flag this immediately rather than letting it be answered casually. Worth putting in front of
