The idea (seen 8 Sep 2026): a retrofit bolt for existing roller doors. Clone the existing remote
code once, throw out the old remotes, and the bolt only unlocks from a secure signal from the
homeowner's phone (fingerprint or app). It pulses the motor on the same frequency after
retracting, so the door never tries to open while locked (which burns motors out). DIY
install, no new motor, no wiring a motherboard. Competitors (BND and others) replace the
whole motor and lock for about $1,800; nobody does a retrofit. One old American patent
exists (about 20 years old). The pitch is deterrence, not "unbreakable".

Plan he has set (seen 8 Sep 2026): 1) register the IP first (patent, China considered on
20 Aug), 2) fit it to his own roller door, 3) trial with a local roller door installer,
4) market it, then go to BND and let them buy it. No government subsidies (insurance and
bureaucracy). Jaiah will help with the website once the IP is registered. He has been working
the plan in a Claude project with Skill creator; ask him to export it into raw/ when ready.
