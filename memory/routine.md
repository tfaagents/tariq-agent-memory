## How he uses the calendar
His calendar is his to-do list. Entries like "Make offer email on Algester" (Mon 8am),
"Prepare questions for Mullins meeting", "Check team hours with Heather", "Decide if you
want to email Daniel cease and desist", "Read Desari email" (6:45pm), "Tidy up accounts
loans once PC13 comes" are tasks he set himself, mostly at 8am. Treat an 8am entry with a
verb as a task to check on, not a meeting.
