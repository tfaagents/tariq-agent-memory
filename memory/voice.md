**Opening:** Almost always "Hi [Name]," (sometimes "HI [Name],"). "Salams Bro/Uncle/Abba"
for family and Muslim contacts. No opener at all when replying fast to Clay or Kendal.

**Closing:** "Kind Regards" (no comma) then his signature block, nearly every email. A few
end "Regards," with the full credential signature ("Tariq Ali | B. Urban Development,
B. Construction Management") for formal external contacts.

**Length:** Short and clipped. One or two sentences per email, rarely more than three or
four. Fires off a direct question or instruction and stops.

**Tone:** Blunt and transactional with staff ("please proceed", "please review", "could you
complete what's requested"). Warmer with people he knows: "Hope you are well my friend",
"hope your week is going well mate", "Love your diligence as always 😊". Humour and
sarcasm freely: "Yay another win after 1 year lol".

**Numbers:** States figures precisely, no hedging: dollar amounts, dates, account numbers
straight into the text. Project data as short bullet lists with clean headers; the prose
around them stays terse.

**Punctuation:** Light on commas, fragments common ("That's my 2cents, please keep me
informed"). Uses "?" freely, stacks emojis for tone (😊🤣😂😬), "lol" and "inshallah" as
softeners.

**Phrases he uses:** "Please see below", "Could you kindly", "Thanks for", "Not sure if",
"Happy to", "More then happy" (his spelling, keep it), "attain" for "attached" (his
typo; do not reproduce in a draft), "my 2cents", "asap", "FYI".

**Never:** long paragraphs, formal throat-clearing, markdown tables, "Sincerely", "Best,",
hedged instructions. Asks straight and stops.

## Corrections
When he replaces a draft with his own wording, add an entry: date, who it was to, what was
suggested (one line), what he actually sent (one line), the lesson.
