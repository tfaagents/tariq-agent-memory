## How TFA prices and runs a D&C tender
- **Clay's model, "TFA Immediate Plan: Win D&C Negotiated Contracts" (27 Sep, from
  claywhart@gmail.com, subject "Randal"):** D&C baseline price + qualifications/exclusions +
  20% design contingency + BA/TFA fees. "We win the job if we stay within our original
  proposal. The design contingency comes down as site testing is completed and quotes firm up
  scope and price." The 20% is a starting number meant to shrink, not a margin. Tariq, 1 Oct:
  "Sounds like a good plan mate. Lets win some work". (seen 1 Oct 2026)
- **Who does what (Tariq, 23 Sep):** "Kendal can assist with compiling the tender submission,
  but the construction, plans, quantities and estimating review need to come from the project
  team." The review meeting is Clay and Veena's; his gate is the price before submission: "I'm
  more so present to review prior to submission of price and understand your teams review of
  inclusions, exclusions and price point reasoning" (22 Sep). (seen 23 Sep 2026)
- **Checklist for an estimator's price**, from Clay's 16 point query list to Abi on
  Collingwood (28 Sep, "I haven't done a measure or checked rates. This is a high level check
  only."). Every line gets the same three questions: is it measured, is it market rate, is
  there a quote behind it. The usual gaps: submittals/insurance; prelims (crane for panels and
  steel, traffic control, survey set-out and as-con, CCTV, testing Level 1/CBR/concrete,
  QLeave, ESC maintenance, "$3k silt only?"); light rates (scaffold, reo supply only?,
  formwork, post tension); tanks (Atlan at $1,000 and $10,000/m3); retaining walls good enough
  as a vehicle crash barrier; unmeasured column verticals and screw piers; civil remediation,
  Level 1 fill (150 kPa, H1 reactivity) and uncontrolled fill; lift quote; temp battering for
  rig, truck and crane access; upper level childcare certification (safety screens, stair
  gates, fire protection); linemarking, wheelstops, bollards. (seen 28 Sep 2026)
