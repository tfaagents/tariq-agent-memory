## The case they are making, in the draft

  asbestos containing material is in the uncontrolled waste mass.
- The owners stopped the development themselves years ago.
- **Epic Environmental**, the vendor's own consultant, calls Zone 3b the "uncontrolled
  waste mass" and the "highest potential contamination risk onsite".
- About **104,500m3** of material to excavate across Zones 2, 3a and 3b, roughly
  **67,500m3** of it uncontrolled waste mass in Zone 3b alone.
- Groundwater exceeds the assessment criteria and **PFAS** is identified, with unresolved
  data gaps. Disposal needs permits, truck manifests and weighbridge reconciliation.
- Their modelled total exposure is **$55 to $60 million**. Their own draft says plainly
  this is a modelled scenario and not a figure in the RAP. Do not repeat the $55-60m as
  a fact; it is their model.
- Seven attachments: the report, then six RAP extracts (p.175 EMR/CLR certificate, p.10
  zones, p.25 ACM auditor, p.33 PFAS, pp.40-43 quantities and permits, p.61 aerial image).
