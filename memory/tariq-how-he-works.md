## Pace and polish
- Fast and direct. He has "lost the filter of making things look pretty" and wants a bot
  that does the polish. Single touch: review, send. (seen 8 Sep 2026; see rules.md 2026-09-08)
- Reads and replies on his phone between things. Invoices all day in the inbox. (seen 8 Sep 2026)
- Hates relying on people. Wants to build around himself so nothing stops when someone
  leaves. "Go sort this out, come back to me." (seen 8 Sep 2026)
- Wants a perfect outcome from inception, then worries it holds him back; wants agents
  that are good and transferable to whatever model is best next. (seen 8 Sep 2026)
- Pressure tests security by asking for things he should not get (staff hours, a
  password, another person's login). The right answer is a plain "not in anything I can
  read" or "no path for that", never a guess. (seen 8 Sep 2026)
- **His calendar is his to-do list, and he does not know where any other list lives.** To
  Kendal, 3:25pm 15 Sep 2026, on a Monday.com item: "its not on my list, but **where is even
  my list?**" Monday.com is not connected, so items on a board he never opens are invisible
  to him. Treat any "did you see the reminder" question as probably no. The Heritage
  Building Upgrade tender due 18 Sep came to light the same way. See [[routine]].
- **He asks where a number came from before he lets you use it.** Telegram, 16 Sep 2026
  5:25am, after being offered a GRV against cost on Dan Street: "**Where will you get the
  info from?**" Name the thread, the person and the date before you put a figure in front
  of him. Never imply you hold a model you do not.
- Anything ambiguous he wants face to face, not written: "I'll leave it to you to come see
  me and discuss follow up queries" (14 Sep 2026, to Clay on the Ecco cross over), and
  "we can chat more when your back" on the apprentice supervision question the same
  morning. He answers email fast, but parks the real decision for the catch up.

## Ownership and delegation
- One task, one owner, no cc'ing everyone: see rules.md 2026-09-14. He takes the reason to
  the person who owns it rather than the person who broke it: the 15 Sep 2026 folder
  structures email went to **Clay**, not to Mauricio, Veena or Daniel.
- A tracker is not an owner: see rules.md 2026-09-23 ("not to take ownership of executing
  the systems for you", to Clay). Never move a job to the person chasing it.
- On questions that belong to someone else's lane: "**your asking me but i thought you
  were managing the estimators respones?**" (22 Sep 2026, to Kendal on Forvm @ Hillcrest.)
  Do not bring him a decision that is already delegated.
- On meetings he does not need to be in: "**I don't know if I'm required in the meeting if
  its something you and Veena can review? I'm more so present to review prior to
  submission of price and understand your teams review of inclusions, exclusions and price
  point reasoning?**" (22 Sep 2026, to Clay on Collingwood Park.) His gate is the price and
  the reasoning **before it goes out**, not the working meeting that produces it.
- On staff being across their own work: "**I just need her to be across all her aspects
  without always saying she is behind.** I appreciate you doing this, please don't stop
  either its good." (22 Sep 2026, to Kendal about Heather and the Christmas shutdown
  projections.) He lets someone else cover the work, but notices who owns it.
- On people breaking under a new structure: "**If people struggle or break under the
  structure, leave that with me to manage and reallocate. What we can't keep doing is
  pushing deadlines back or continually shifting priorities.**" (23 Sep 2026, to Clay, who
  had predicted two of the team would break.) He takes the people problem, the deadline holds.
- Titles are earned in front of the person, not handed over. On Daniel: "I don't want to
  change his title to CA yet maybe keep it for the next year as Project Coordinator in
  training for CA or CA Cadet? **So he is aware he has to work towards it?**" (14 Sep
  2026, to Clay.)
- **He sets the benchmark, not the recruiter.** "It's a hot market, everyone states they can
  assist, but then put forward poor candidates. We don't wish to waste each others time."
  (10 Sep 2026, to Frontline.) The CA brief to Frontline (21 Sep 2026) tells her the
  interview questions, referees, gaps and risks, then asks her to confirm receipt. He hands
  over the standard, not the judgement.
- He invites people to ask big and filters afterwards. To Heather on the new office wish
  list: "not everything will be accepted fyi it's a wish list so make sure you **keep
  wishing** hahah", then one flat no (no dogs in the office) with a counter-offer (an
  office cat) (17 Sep 2026). He rarely kills an idea without leaving something in its place.

## Systems and standards
- **Foundations before scale**, folder layout and templates are the standard: see rules.md
  2026-09-15. His words: "**as we scale the issue is going to amplify** ... **I hold this
  topic with importance in scale and growth having the foundations right prior to
  scaling**" (15 Sep 2026, 4:06pm, to Clay). The shape: warm opener ("I hope your day
  playing with pineapples went well mate haha"), the problem plainly, then the catch up agenda.
- On systems that never ship: "**I don't want things sitting in development
  indefinitely. I want a tangible and measurable rollout where we can clearly see what is
  complete, what is going live, what is outstanding, what is holding it up and who is
  responsible for getting it finished.**" (22 Sep 2026, to Clay cc Kendal, "Systems and
  Operations - Review Before Our Catch Up".) He wants **2 to 4 weeks shifted heavily to
  implementation and rollout** across eleven areas: Monday/task accountability, development
  management systems and templates, weekly development reporting, CA/PM/VA workflows, VA
  management and checking, OneDrive/SharePoint filing and document control, procedures and
  the Scribe and video training library, the project document and template suite, cost codes
  and job costing, project setup and closeout, and procurement. **Status per area, with a
  blocker and an owner, is the format he wants anything reported back in.**
- On finishing: "**I just want to support in ensuring it gets to 100% asap in lieu of
  sitting at 80% complete like whatever I have built in the past 2-4 years.**" (23 Sep
  2026, to Clay.) 80 percent is his named failure mode; report percentage complete, not a
  status word.
- "**We can build workflows, but they have no value if the team isn't consistently
  implementing them.**" (23 Sep 2026, to Clay.) Documented, filed and in use is the test.
- He measures TFA against the best he sees: on another builder's handover document list,
  "I'm wanting to know where you team is at with this information?" to Clay, and asked
  Kendal to make ours the same (10 Sep 2026). Forwarding Ray White's "Final Release -
  SmartStores@EagleFarm" to Kendal, 7:44am 21 Sep 2026: "FYI **update styles for our sites
  in future**." Competitor campaigns are a template, not noise.
- On how he wants the AI built: "An AI builder could easily create 25 separate bots when
  what you really need is probably 7-8 coordinated agents sitting on one common operating
  system." (11 Sep 2026, to Jaiah.) He rewrote Jaiah's list himself because it mixed
  outcomes, integrations, examples and risk levels together. When you propose something,
  separate the outcome from the integration from the risk. See [[agent-roadmap]].
- Staff monitoring only ever as a transparent, compliance-approved module: see rules.md
  2026-09-11. He also said staff working-style notes should "combine information they
  provide with legitimate management observations rather than inventing psychological
  profiles" (11 Sep 2026, to Jaiah). Hold him to this if a staff-monitoring job lands.
