# Hiring

Consolidated 2 Oct 2026 from projects.md. Interns and apprentices are in [[projects-team-admin]].

## Contract Administrator (via Alicia McGregor, Frontline Construction Recruitment)
- **Band for the role: 120-160k** (16 Sep, "120-160k"; 21 Sep: "Happy to increase our pool from
  120k to 160k for the right candidate and qualifications."). (seen 21 Sep 2026)
- **21 Sep 7:48am he sent the full brief**; Alicia confirmed receipt ("I will work on finding you
  stand out candidates"). The benchmark: **5+ years as a Contracts Administrator**; tertiary
  qualification in Construction Management or similar, **completed before the CA role**; CA
  experience on **ground-up building work**; **Brisbane/SEQ subcontractor knowledge**; has been
  the **sole CA on projects**, reporting **directly to a Project Manager**; light commercial
  delivery; site exposure highly regarded. (seen 21 Sep 2026)
- **Systems not negotiable:** Procore proficiency essential (TFA's primary platform), Bluebeam, MS
  Project, Outlook, OneDrive, advanced Excel. TFA runs ProScan (Procore to MYOB) and Monday.com.
  Contracts AS2124, AS4000, ABIC, MBA. BIF Act (Qld) progress claims and payment schedules, QBCC
  licensing and warranties. (seen 21 Sep 2026)
- **Who he does not want:** "Do not forward QS candidates" unless exceptional; avoid heavily
  institutional or corporate-styled candidates: "We run lean and direct, with minimal layers
  between decision-making and delivery. Candidates coming from highly resourced Tier 1
  environments often struggle with the pace and personal accountability required here." (seen
  21 Sep 2026)
- **Load:** TFA projects run $5-15m; a CA runs 2-3 projects, no more than $15m accumulatively.
  (seen 21 Sep 2026)
- **What the recruiter must do first:** interview personally (video or face to face); ask his CA
  interview questions and send the answers in full; reference check 1-2 referees and summarise;
  flag gaps (Procore fluency, BIF Act, degree, distance from the office, procurement and package
  letting, cultural fit) and risks (tenure, Tier 1 low adaptability, over-delegation, no financial
  ownership). He sent the CA Position Description, CA Interview Questions, TFA Capability
  Statement, Newsletter Issue No.3, the team video (vimeo.com/847691927) and an Instagram reel.
  "I'm happy to keep testing if they hit closer to our benchmark." (seen 21 Sep 2026)
- **1 Oct:** Alicia's Senior Contract Administrator candidate wants $50m+ projects. Tariq: "we
  are doing 200m projects ... I presume they are not suitable hence hiding behind the figure.
  Don't tell them. Show them". She sent the CV and offered a meeting (3:26pm); he replied "no
  attachment". (seen 1 Oct 2026)
- **2 Oct 7:36am he declined that candidate:** "Done some background research no good sorry, wants
  an entire team under them and doesn't last long in businesses. Happy to revisit any other
  candidates." Search still open. (seen 2 Oct 2026)
- For any draft on this thread: the TFA website (tfaconstructions.com.au) is still "currently
  under construction" in his own words. (seen 21 Sep 2026)
