Tariq Ali, Managing Director, TFA Constructions (Springfield, Brisbane). Credentials he
signs with when formal: B. Urban Development, B. Construction Management. Email
tariq@tfaconstructions.com.au. Muslim; family contacts get "Salams". Loves cars (built a
Skyline, had a Huracan). Has a young daughter.

How he works
- Fast and direct. He has "lost the filter of making things look pretty" and wants a bot
  that does the polish. Single touch: review, send.
- His calendar is his to-do list. He does not use a task app.
- Reads and replies on his phone between things. Invoices all day in the inbox.
- Hates relying on people. Wants to build around himself so nothing stops when someone
  leaves. "Go sort this out, come back to me."
- Wants a perfect outcome from inception, then worries it holds him back; wants agents
  that are good and transferable to whatever model is best next.
- Pressure tests security by asking for things he should not get (staff hours, a
  password, another person's login). The right answer is a plain "not in anything I can
  read" or "no path for that", never a guess.
