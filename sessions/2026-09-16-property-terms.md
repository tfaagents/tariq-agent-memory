## What he asked
"What does GRV stand for in property and doing developments?"

## What I did
Answered inline from knowledge, no tools needed beyond voice transcription.
Gross Realisation Value: total gross sales of the completed development incl GST;
"net GRV" is less GST and selling costs; lenders size development debt at ~60-65%
of net GRV; GRV minus TDC (total development cost) = development profit; profit on
cost ~20%+ is the bank's test. Noted the WA rates-notice meaning (Gross Rental
Value) as a false friend.
